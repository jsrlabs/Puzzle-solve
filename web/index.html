Puzzle Helper

Photograph the box picture and your loose pieces. The app finds two loose pieces that belong next to each other and shows you how to join them. Everything runs on your device; photos are never uploaded.

1. The box picture

Photograph the picture on the box from straight on, filling the frame. Then adjust the crop so only the puzzle picture is inside.

2. The loose pieces

Spread pieces picture-side up on a plain surface that contrasts with them (a dark cloth or a sheet of paper). Make sure no pieces touch, and photograph from straight above in good light.
