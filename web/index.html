Puzzle Helper

Photograph the box picture and your loose pieces. The app tells you which piece to place next and where it goes, always next to pieces already on your board, starting from a corner. Everything runs on your device; photos are never uploaded.

1. The box picture

Photograph the picture on the box. It doesn't need to be straight: the app finds the picture and straightens it.

2. Your table

Spread pieces picture-side up on a plain mat or cloth that contrasts with them, not touching each other. Photograph from straight above in good light. Include the puzzle you've started (e.g. the border) in the same photo and the app recognises the pieces already in place.
